# OMEGA Lab

**Omnimodal Multi-Embodiment Generalist Agents** · The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

Our research connects multimodal perception, intent and motion modeling, and autonomous decision-making in physical systems and digital workflows. We study how agents perceive, model, reason and act, from robot policies and world models to tool use, multi-agent workflows and the safety of embodied systems.

### Research directions

- Multimodal Perception — representing the physical world
- Robot Learning & Manipulation — learning policies for action
- World Models & Prediction — modeling how the world changes
- Vision-Language Navigation — grounding language in space
- Intent & Decision-Making — understanding behavior and decisions
- Multi-Agent Systems — coordinating agents and tools
- Embodied AI Safety — stress-testing and hardening embodied agents
